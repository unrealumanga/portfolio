SHT.003Studio & Process

Hethusha Umanga Seneviratne is an experiential designer based in Dubai, working at the intersection of brand strategy and spatial storytelling — shaping how people move through, and feel inside, a built environment.

01 / CONCEPT & NARRATIVE

Concept

Brand strategy is translated into a spatial story — zoning, sequence and moments of arrival that give a brand a place to live in three dimensions.

02 / DESIGN & COORDINATION

Coordination

Concepts are resolved into buildable drawings and photoreal visuals, coordinated across architects, fabricators and contractors ahead of build.

03 / BUILD & HANDOVER

Delivery

On-site through fit-out and installation, resolving the inevitable — until the space is show-ready and handed over.
